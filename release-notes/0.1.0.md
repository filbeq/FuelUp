First public pre-release of FuelUp: Italian fuel prices on a map, from the
open data of the Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy (MIMIT).

**What it does**

- Every fuel station in Italy on the map, grouped when zoomed out, with the
  lowest price of each group.
- Choose your fuel (petrol, diesel, LPG, methane, LNG) and self-service or
  served: stations are marked cheap, average or expensive compared with the
  stations around them.
- Station details: all prices, when each was last reported, navigation with
  your maps app.
- "Near me": the cheapest or nearest stations within 5, 10 or 20 km.
- Search for stations, brands and towns; favourite stations.
- Works offline with the last downloaded prices; light and dark theme;
  English and Italian.

**Good to know**

- Prices are those in force at 08:00 on the date shown in the app, updated
  once a day: not real time.
- This is an early test version for Android 8 and newer. The future Play Store
  version will be a separate app with a new name.
- Unofficial app, not affiliated with MIMIT.
