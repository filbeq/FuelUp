**New**

- Prices straight from the Ministry: since mid-October the Ministry (MIMIT)
  only answers connections from Italy, which stopped FuelUp's daily published
  copy from updating. FuelUp now downloads the Ministry's files itself and
  prepares them on your phone, with the same checks as before. It takes a few
  seconds more, at most once a day, and uses about 7.5 MB instead of 1.5 MB
  when the Ministry has newer prices than FuelUp's copy.
- Settings → Data shows where the prices on screen come from: "downloaded
  from the Ministry (MIMIT)" or "FuelUp's published copy". About explains
  both.
- Outside Italy (or on a foreign SIM, a VPN or a foreign Wi-Fi) the Ministry
  doesn't answer, so FuelUp uses its published copy, which may be older:
  the date shows how old the prices are, with "Update delayed" when newer
  prices should exist, and Settings explains why.

**Fixed**

- Station lists ("Near me" and the area list) now jump back to the first
  station when you change the order, radius or fuel, search a town or tap
  "Search this area", instead of staying on the station that was in view.
- Text no longer turns near-white in the light theme on phones that apply
  a dark mode to apps (e.g. Xiaomi's "dark mode for apps"): the station
  names, the search, the date, the fuel button and the language setting are
  readable again.

**Updating**

- Installs over 0.2.0: your favourites, fuel choice and settings are kept.
